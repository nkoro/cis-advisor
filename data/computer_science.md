# Major in Computer Science

* [Requirements](#requirementstextcontainer)
* [Four-Year Plan of Study](#fouryearplanofstudytextcontainer)
* [Learning Outcomes](#learningoutcomestextcontainer)

All Computer Science major must complete 66-67 units of shared major requirements and an additional 15-22 units of either elective courses or track required courses for a total of 81-89 units. A minimum of 30 major units must be taken at Towson University. Students cannot double major in Computer Science and Computer Information Systems, or in Computer Science and Information Technology.

All Computer Science major must complete 66-67 units of shared major requirements and an additional 15-22 units of either elective courses or track required courses for a total of 81-89 units. A minimum of 30 major units must be taken at Towson University. Students cannot double major in Computer Science and Computer Information Systems, or in Computer Science and Information Technology.
